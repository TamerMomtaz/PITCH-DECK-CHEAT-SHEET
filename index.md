## 🎯 DESIGN PRINCIPLES FOR ALL SLIDES

### **Typography:**
- **Headlines:** 44-54pt, bold
- **Body text:** 24-32pt, regular
- **Never go below 18pt** (can't be read from back of room)

### **Content:**
- **Maximum 6 words per line**
- **Maximum 6 lines per slide**
- **Use visuals over text** whenever possible

### **Visuals:**
- **High-quality images** (no pixelated photos)
- **Simple icons** for concepts
- **Graphs/charts** for data
- **Screenshots** for product demo

### **Colors:**
- Use **RootRise brand colors** consistently
- **High contrast** (dark text on light, or light on dark)
- **Avoid red/green** combinations (colorblind audience)

### **Animation:**
- **Keep it minimal** - simple fades only
- **Don't distract** from your message
- **Practice timing** so animations don't slow you down

## ❓ ANTICIPATED QUESTIONS & HOW TO ANSWER

### **Q: "What if big consulting firms lower prices?"**
A: "Traditional consulting doesn't scale. Their model is people-hours. To serve 1,000 SMEs they'd need 500 consultants. We can serve 1,000 with a team of 20 because of AI. Their cost structure won't allow them to compete at our price point while maintaining quality."

### **Q: "Why will SMEs trust an AI over human consultants?"**
A: "They don't have to choose. We're not replacing humans - we're augmenting them. Our AI does the time-consuming analysis. Human experts do the strategic guidance. That combination is what makes us affordable AND effective. Plus, our first customers are already seeing results - that's the best trust-builder."

### **Q: "How do you acquire customers cost-effectively?"**
A: "Three ways: First, I have direct relationships with 200+ plant managers and SME owners from my professional network. That's our seed base. Second, business associations love referring us because we help their members. Third, our first customers become case studies that attract others. Our CAC of €500 is already validated."

### **Q: "What's your defensibility if someone copies you?"**
A: "Four moats: One, our AI gets smarter with every diagnostic - network effects. Two, we're building deep integration with Egyptian business ecosystems. Three, I have 15 years of practitioner credibility that can't be copied. Four, first-mover advantage - by the time competitors launch, we'll have 500 customers and be the category leader."

### **Q: "Why not just sell the software to consultants?"**
A: "We considered that B2B2B model. Problem is, consultants want to maintain high margins. They'd charge SMEs €50K and keep €45K, defeating our mission of accessibility. Going direct-to-SME ensures we deliver maximum value at minimum cost. Plus, SME owners don't want consultants - they want results."

### **Q: "Can you really scale with just software? Don't you need consultants?"**
A: "Yes and no. The diagnostic and roadmap are 100% automated - that's the AI's strength. For customers on our Transformation tier who want hands-on guidance, we bring in vetted local consultants on-demand. They're not our employees - they're a marketplace. This keeps our costs variable and lets us scale efficiently."

### **Q: "What happens if the Egyptian economy struggles?"**
A: "Ironically, downturns accelerate our business. When times are tough, SMEs need to get more efficient FAST. Our platform helps them cut costs, improve margins, and survive. In 2008, business transformation services grew 40% during the recession. We're actually counter-cyclical."

## 💡 PRO TIPS

1. **The 10/20/30 Rule:**
   - 10 slides minimum
   - 20 minutes maximum
   - 30pt font minimum

2. **Start with a Hook:**
   - Personal story
   - Surprising statistic
   - Bold statement
   - Question to audience

3. **Use the Rule of Three:**
   - Human brain loves threes
   - Three reasons, three tiers, three benefits
   - Easier to remember

4. **Tell Stories:**
   - Don't just say "we have customers"
   - Say "Meet Ahmed, who runs a furniture factory..."
   - Stories stick, statistics don't

5. **Practice Q&A:**
   - Have someone grill you
   - Record yourself answering
   - Know your weak points

6. **Bring Energy:**
   - Stand, don't sit (if possible)
   - Use hand gestures
   - Vary your tone
   - Show passion

7. **End with CTA:**
   - Clear call-to-action
   - What happens next?
   - Leave them excited
